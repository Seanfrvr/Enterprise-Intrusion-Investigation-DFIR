# Case Overview

> Documentation status: in development.

## Scope

This case study examines an enterprise intrusion scenario through a defensive security and digital forensics lens. The published version will focus on independently authored analysis, investigative methodology, validated artefacts, and defensive recommendations.

## Investigation objectives

- Reconstruct the intrusion sequence from available telemetry.
- Assess account activity, execution, persistence, credential access, lateral movement, collection, and command-and-control indicators.
- Separate directly observed evidence from interpretation and hypotheses.
- Identify detection opportunities and incident-response priorities.

## Evidence standard

Each conclusion will be linked to a source artefact where publication is permitted. Unknown timestamps, identities, and outcomes will remain explicitly unconfirmed.
