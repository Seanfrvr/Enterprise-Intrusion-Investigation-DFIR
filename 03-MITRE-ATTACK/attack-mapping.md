> Documentation status: in development. Technique mappings will be validated against the current MITRE ATT&CK knowledge base before publication.

## Mapping standard

Mappings will include the technique ID, name, tactic, supporting observation, confidence, and limitations. Techniques will not be assigned solely because a tool or command appeared in the environment.
