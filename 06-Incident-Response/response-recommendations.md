# Incident Response Recommendations

> Documentation status: in development.

## Response priorities

Recommendations will be tied to validated findings and organised by containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned.

## Areas for assessment

- Identity containment and credential hygiene.
- Endpoint isolation and forensic preservation.
- Web-server and application review.
- Directory-services integrity.
- Centralised logging and tamper resistance.
- Validation of potential data exposure.
